# Behavioral / STAR Story Bank

Anthropic doesn't use Amazon's Leadership Principles, but interviewers are generally probing for: **ownership & judgment under ambiguity, clear communication, collaboration, honesty (including about mistakes/limitations), and genuine care about doing the work well/safely.** Map your Amazon stories to these rather than to LPs directly.

## Story template
```
### [Short title]
**Situation:**
**Task:**
**Action:** (be specific — "I" not "we", show your actual decisions)
**Result:** (quantify if possible)
**What I'd map this to:** ownership / ambiguity / conflict / failure+learning / cross-team collaboration / technical depth / mentorship
```

## Stories to draft (aim for 6-8 total, each should cover 2+ prompts below)

- [ ] A time you owned something ambiguous with no clear spec
- [ ] A time you disagreed with a decision (peer or manager) — how you handled it
- [ ] A time you shipped something that broke / a mistake you made and how you handled it
- [ ] A time you had to influence without authority (cross-team, no reporting line)
- [ ] A time you simplified something complex or pushed back on unnecessary complexity
- [ ] A time you mentored/helped a teammate grow
- [ ] Your most technically challenging project — be ready to go deep
- [ ] A time you had to make a call with incomplete information under time pressure

## "Why Anthropic" — prep this explicitly, don't wing it
- [ ] 60-second version: why this company specifically, not "I like AI"
- [ ] What do you actually know about Anthropic's approach to safety/interpretability that you find interesting?
- [ ] 3-4 questions to ask interviewers that show genuine curiosity about the mission/team
